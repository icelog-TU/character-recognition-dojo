# Lesson Cast Reference Assets

This file lists approved reference assets for recurring lesson-image cast identities.

Use this file with `docs/LESSON_VISUAL_CAST_SOP.md` and the Visual Continuity Ledger in `docs/CURRICULUM_LEDGER.md`.

## Current Approved Cast References

| Role | Asset Path | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist godmother / `乾媽` | `public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/godmother.webp` | Very tall adult woman in the protagonist girl's trusted family-friend circle. Use for `乾媽`, especially height-focused sentences such as `乾媽的身高很高`. Keep distinct from `我媽媽` and `老師`: deep green or blue-green long jacket/top, short or neat medium-length hair, warm but crisp, no teacher props or classroom posture. |
| 小月 / recurring girl classmate | `public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/xiaoyue.webp` | Same age band as the protagonist girl; cute fixed classmate with long softly curly dark chestnut hair, small crescent-moon hair clip, lavender cardigan, pale cream shirt, teal pleated skirt, white socks, violet shoes, and a plain small school bag when useful. Keep distinct from the protagonist girl: no short bob, pink hair clip, pink jacket, blue skirt, or pink shoes. |
| 小光 / recurring boy classmate | `public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/xiaoguang.webp` | Same age band as the protagonist girl; fixed boy classmate with neat round glasses, tidy short black side-parted hair, model-student look, white short-sleeve shirt, navy knit vest, khaki shorts, white socks, brown school shoes, and a plain dark green school bag when useful. Keep distinct from the existing `你` young boy classmate: no sky-blue shirt, green shorts, blue shoes, or orange backpack. |

## Editor To Production Wording

When a sentence uses `乾媽`, Editor should include this in `imageNotes` or the Production handoff:

```text
Use the approved recurring protagonist godmother / 乾媽 reference asset: public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/godmother.webp. She is a very tall adult woman in the protagonist girl's trusted family-friend circle, clearly taller than 我媽媽 and 老師, warm but crisp, with short or neat medium-length hair and a deep green or blue-green long jacket/top. Do not make her look like 媽媽 or 老師; no yellow/coral-red mother palette, no classroom clothing, no book, no pointer, no teaching posture.
```

When a sentence uses `小月`, Editor should include this in `imageNotes` or the Production handoff:

```text
Use the approved recurring girl classmate 小月 reference asset: public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/xiaoyue.webp. She is the protagonist girl's same-age classmate, with long softly curly dark chestnut hair, a small crescent-moon hair clip, lavender cardigan, pale cream shirt, teal pleated skirt, white socks, and violet shoes. Keep her visually distinct from the protagonist girl; do not give 小月 the protagonist's short bob, pink hair clip, pink jacket, blue skirt, or pink shoes.
```

When a sentence uses `小光`, Editor should include this in `imageNotes` or the Production handoff:

```text
Use the approved recurring boy classmate 小光 reference asset: public/assets/reference/lesson-cast/xiaoguang.webp. He is the protagonist girl's same-age classmate, with neat round glasses, tidy short black side-parted hair, a white short-sleeve shirt, navy knit vest, khaki shorts, white socks, brown school shoes, and a plain dark green school bag when useful. Keep him visually distinct from the existing `你` young boy classmate; do not use sky-blue shirt, green shorts, blue shoes, or an orange backpack.
```
